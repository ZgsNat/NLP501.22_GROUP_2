## Registered decision

**Silver is secondary; the human-labelled sample is the primary validation evidence.**

The two humans cover every one of the 94 A/B disagreements. This is independent double annotation, not joint adjudication; disagreement-heavy sampling makes these κ values deliberately conservative relative to the full 600-pair corpus.
