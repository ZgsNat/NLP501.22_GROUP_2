# Djinni Real-World Information Extraction Benchmark

Status: **HUMAN_ANNOTATION_PENDING**.

The frozen sample contains 12 pilot profiles and 50 test profiles, disjoint from the production and ranking-holdout pools by candidate ID and normalized text hash. The test split remains sealed in `data/eval/djinni_extraction_benchmark_manifest.json`.

The previous files named `human_extraction_a.json`, `human_extraction_b.json`, and `djinni_extraction_reference_50.json` were generated by one regex/taxonomy script. Their disagreements and adjudication were also generated. They therefore do not constitute independent human annotations, IAA, or Human Gold. They are retained under weak/heuristic filenames for provenance and debugging only.

## Registered human protocol

- Two distinct people annotate all 50 test profiles independently in differently ordered offline kits.
- Neither person sees system output, weak labels, benchmark scores, or the other's labels.
- Validation is fail-closed on exact document coverage and hashes, raw-text offsets, taxonomy/OOV schema, distinct annotator IDs, and provenance flags.
- IAA is computed before adjudication with one-to-one token-IoU alignment at the registered threshold of 0.5.
- A third person resolves only the revealed conflicts. No heuristic adjudication is allowed.
- The primary E0-E2 benchmark and downstream impact analysis remain blocked until the frozen Human Gold artifact exists.
- E3 remains **NOT RUN** unless a quantitative record includes the actual model, trace, prompt/configuration, token use, and outputs.

Run `python -m app.eval.djinni_extraction_annotation prepare-kits` from `backend/` to build the two local HTML kits. After both exports exist, run the `iaa` command to validate them and create the conflict-only adjudication kit.

Historical numbers such as κ = 0.818, span F1 = 0.991, and E2 micro-F1 = 0.943 are generated weak-label diagnostics. They must not appear as human or headline results.
