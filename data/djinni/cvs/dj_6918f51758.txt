Time Tracker - This is a time-tracker web application. For full-time employees, records are created automatically by the button click with a duration of 8 hours, and for part-time employees, a timer is started. It is also possible to submit requests for vacation and sick leave, which is confirmed by the relevant person. Workers with certain privileges have an open access to the list of users with search, sorting, pagination and export to XLSX / PDF.
Stack: React, Redux, RxJs, TypeScript, HTML/CSS/SCSS, GraphQL, ASP.Net, Dapper, MS SQL.
