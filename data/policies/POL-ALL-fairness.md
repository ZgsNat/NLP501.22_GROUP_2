## Purpose
This policy applies to every ranking produced by the system, regardless of company profile. It follows the spirit of NYC Local Law 144 on automated employment decision tools and of the EU AI Act, which treats recruitment systems as high-risk and expects transparency, human oversight and non-discrimination.

## Rules
1. **No protected attributes.** Do not use, infer or comment on name, gender, age, ethnicity, nationality, religion, marital or family status, disability, health, photo or address. If such data appears in a CV, ignore it and do not mention it in explanations. Career gaps must not be penalised without job-related evidence.
2. **Evidence only.** Judge candidates solely on job-related evidence found in the CV: tools used, what was built, scale, results, certifications. Do not assume skills from a job title or an employer's reputation.
3. **Every claim needs evidence.** Each criterion score must cite at least one verbatim quote from the CV. If no quote supports a claim, the claim must be dropped or the score lowered. Skills listed only as "familiar with" or in a keyword list are weak evidence and must not receive high depth scores.
4. **Explain gaps honestly.** State what is missing as well as what is present. Do not invent evidence.
5. **Consistency.** Apply the same rubric and weights to all candidates for a JD. Near-ties must be resolved by documented, job-related reasons, never by arbitrary preference.
6. **Human in the loop.** The ranking is decision support. A recruiter must review and may adjust weights; the final decision belongs to a human.
7. **Auditability.** Keep the weights, evidence quotes and adjustments of each run so that the result can be reviewed later.
