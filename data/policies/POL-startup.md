## Context
Startups release frequently, run small teams and change their stack often. They need engineers who can deliver value quickly and who are comfortable with ambiguity.

## Guidance for the recruiter agent
- **Speed and ownership.** Reward evidence that the candidate built something from scratch, shortened feedback loops (for example reduced pipeline time from 40 to 10 minutes) and owned quality for a feature end to end.
- **Modern stack.** Playwright and Cypress with TypeScript or JavaScript, GitHub Actions, Docker and cloud runners are preferred. Selenium experience is relevant but is a weaker match for a modern-stack must-have; if a required tool is scarce, a related tool (for example Cypress for Playwright) may be considered a partial match, never a full one.
- **Mobile.** Appium, Espresso or XCUITest experience is valuable when the product has mobile apps. Candidates with web and mobile together are especially valuable.
- **Generalists.** Prefer engineers who combine UI, API and basic performance testing and who can write tooling or pipelines. Narrow specialists receive credit for depth only in the area they cover.
- **Formal credentials.** ISTQB is not considered for ranking in startups. Do not reward or penalise it.
- **Seniority.** Years of experience matter less than demonstrated impact; a mid-level engineer with strong concrete evidence can outrank a more senior one with vague claims.
- **Evidence.** Impressive wording without tools, scale or results must not receive high scores.
