## About the role
We are an IT outsourcing company with a variety of international clients. We are looking for an Automation Test Engineer to join one of our delivery teams. The exact project is still being confirmed and may change.

## Responsibilities
- Create and maintain automated tests for client applications.
- Work with the team and the client to decide what to automate.
- Use modern tools and practices to improve quality and delivery speed.
- Report on test progress and results to the project manager.
- Support other testers and share knowledge when needed.

## Requirements
- Several years of experience in testing or a related field.
- Good automation skills and the ability to learn new technologies quickly.
- Experience with modern tools and frameworks suitable for the client's needs.
- Understanding of the software development life cycle and Agile ways of working.
- Good communication skills and a team-player attitude.
- English is a plus, or maybe required depending on the client.
- Willingness to work flexible hours when the client needs it.

## Nice to have
- Certifications or domain knowledge, depending on the project.
