## About the role
We are a product company with 14 engineering teams and a growing quality platform. Each team currently invents its own test setup. We are looking for a Test Automation Architect who will own the automation strategy across teams, design the shared framework and CI quality gates, and coach engineers and QA leads to adopt them.

## Responsibilities
- Define the test automation strategy and the test pyramid for all product teams, including what each layer must cover.
- Design and evolve the shared automation framework and libraries (UI, API, contract) used by many teams, with versioning and clear ownership.
- Own CI quality gates in the delivery pipelines: release criteria, flaky-test policy, performance and security thresholds.
- Design test infrastructure: containerised or Kubernetes-based runners, parallel execution, test environments and data management.
- Introduce consumer-driven contract testing between services.
- Coach and mentor QA engineers and developers; lead a small group of automation engineers.
- Report quality metrics to engineering leadership and influence roadmap decisions.

## Requirements
Must have:
- 8+ years in software testing and test automation, of which 3+ years in a lead or architect role.
- Proven design and delivery of a shared automation framework used by multiple teams (not only your own).
- Hands-on strength in at least one mainstream stack (Java, TypeScript or Python) and in CI/CD (GitLab CI, GitHub Actions or Jenkins).
- Experience defining quality gates that actually block releases.
- Strong communication: able to influence engineers and managers without direct authority.

Nice to have:
- Kubernetes and Docker for test infrastructure.
- Pact or other contract testing at scale.
- Performance testing (k6, JMeter) integrated into pipelines.
- ISTQB Advanced or Expert levels.
