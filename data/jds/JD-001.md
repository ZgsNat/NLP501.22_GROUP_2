## About the role
We are a retail bank technology division building internet and mobile banking services. We are looking for a Senior Automation Test Engineer to own the automated regression and API test coverage of our payment and account platforms.

## Responsibilities
- Design, extend and maintain a Selenium + Java test automation framework (Page Object Model, parallel execution, reporting).
- Build and maintain automated API tests for REST services and integrate them into CI/CD pipelines.
- Define and enforce quality gates in Jenkins or GitLab CI before every release.
- Provide audit-ready test evidence and traceability between requirements, test cases and defects.
- Analyse flaky tests and improve the stability and speed of the suites.
- Mentor junior testers and review their test code.

## Requirements
Must have:
- At least 5 years of experience in software testing, including 3+ years of test automation.
- Strong hands-on experience with Selenium WebDriver and Java.
- Experience with API testing (RestAssured or Postman) and with CI/CD tools such as Jenkins.
- Solid understanding of test design techniques and regression strategy.

Nice to have:
- ISTQB certification (Foundation or higher is preferred).
- Experience with Cucumber/BDD, Docker and Selenium Grid.
- Prior work in banking, payments or other regulated domains.
- Good written English for documentation.
