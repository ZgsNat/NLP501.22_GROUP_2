How I work with teams
I try not to impose a framework. First I read the existing tests and ask the team what hurts. Typical findings are slow pipelines, tests that fail without reason, and tests nobody understands. I propose the smallest platform feature that removes the pain, ship it, measure it, and only then ask other teams to adopt it. When a team has a good reason to deviate, I accept that and document the trade-off rather than force alignment. I measure the platform by outcomes: time from commit to feedback, flaky rate, defect escape rate and how many tests are written by developers rather than by the QA group. Hiring and growing engineers is a large part of the job; I run structured interviews, and I have promoted three engineers to senior level through clear growth plans.
