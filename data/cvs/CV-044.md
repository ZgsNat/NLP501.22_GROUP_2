Professional Profile:
Mobile QA engineer with 4 years of experience testing Android and iOS apps for a retail loyalty application and a banking-lite wallet. Strong in exploratory testing, device compatibility and release verification. Familiar with Appium (not used in production), and I am interested in moving towards mobile test automation.

Employment History:

QA Engineer, Retail loyalty app company
2022/08 – present

I test each release of the customer app on a pool of about 15 real devices and several emulators. I write and maintain around 350 manual test cases covering coupons, point balance, store locator and push notifications, and I run the release checklist with the product owner. I coordinate beta testing through the stores' testing channels and triage the feedback. I check logs with ADB and use Charles Proxy to inspect traffic and to simulate slow networks. For backend checks I send requests with Postman.

A colleague on the platform team once demoed Appium to us and I followed a tutorial to run a sample login test on an emulator; this was a proof of concept in a sandbox project and was never added to our regression process. I have not built or maintained an Appium suite. Our release regression remains manual, and I have not worked with Espresso or XCUITest at all.

Tester, Wallet application company
2020/07 – 2022/07

Functional and regression testing of an Android wallet app: top-up, transfers, transaction history and limits. Wrote test cases and defect reports in Jira, verified fixes and prepared release notes for testers. Took part in security awareness sessions and checked the app for basic issues such as sensitive data in logs.

Support Engineer, Telecom services company
2019/01 – 2020/06

Handled customer tickets for a mobile data service, reproduced issues and escalated them with logs to developers. Moved to QA after a year.

Skills:
Manual testing on Android and iOS, exploratory testing, ADB, Charles Proxy, Postman (basic), Jira, TestRail, device lab management, SQL (basic). Learning: Appium (tutorial level), Java (basic syntax).

Education:
BSc Information Technology
